<Info>
  **Status: dates and commitments are awaiting team approval.** Roadmap items below are organized as outcomes, not promises.
</Info>

## Foundation

- Content creation and encrypted local storage
- Signed creator listings
- Mainnet and isolated testnet configuration
- Onchain transfer verification and replay protection
- Wallet connection and network switching

## Product hardening

- Complete live end-to-end settlement testing
- Commission an independent security review
- Publish production operations and incident-response procedures
- Add durable, scalable storage for multi-instance deployments
- Improve interrupted-transaction and replacement-transaction recovery

## Token readiness

- Publish final contract and administrative controls
- Publish allocations and onchain vesting schedules
- Complete contract audit and remediation
- Publish eligibility and jurisdictional restrictions
- Establish treasury transparency reporting

## Ecosystem expansion

- Document third-party integration interfaces
- Add creator analytics and improved discovery
- Evaluate standards-compatible payment interoperability
- Launch governance only after its scope and safeguards are documented

<Warning>
  Roadmaps can change because of engineering, security, market, or regulatory constraints. A roadmap is not a guarantee of delivery or token value.
</Warning>
