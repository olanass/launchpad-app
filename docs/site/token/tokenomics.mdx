The initial mint destination describes where supply entered circulation mechanics; it does not by itself disclose beneficial ownership, later distribution, liquidity depth, or vesting. Any future allocation breakdown should total **100%** and link to relevant wallets, vesting contracts, or public dashboards.

## Vesting disclosure standard

For every locked allocation, Olanas should publish:

1. the number of tokens allocated;
2. the beneficiary category;
3. the cliff date and duration;
4. the release frequency and end date;
5. whether vesting can be revoked or changed; and
6. the onchain vesting address.

## Treasury reporting

Treasury documentation should identify authorized signers, signature threshold, permitted uses, reporting cadence, and any policy for converting tokens or other assets.
