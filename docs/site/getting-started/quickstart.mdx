---
title: "User quickstart"
description: "A safe first-run guide for creators and buyers."
---

Choose the path that matches what you want to do.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Buy content" icon="cart-shopping">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open an official paywall link">
        Confirm the domain was shared through an official Olanas source. Do not connect a wallet through links sent by unknown accounts.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the requirement">
        Check the network, currency, exact amount, and creator address before approving anything.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect and switch networks">
        Connect a compatible wallet. The application may ask the wallet to switch to Robinhood Chain mainnet (4663).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Approve the transfer">
        Read the wallet confirmation carefully. Olanas does not need your seed phrase or private key.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
        Keep the page open while the transfer is mined and verified. The application retains the pending transaction hash within the current tab to help recover an interrupted download.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Publish content" icon="upload">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect the creator wallet">
        Use the address that should receive buyer payments.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add content and pricing">
        Upload a supported file or enter text, then choose a configured currency and exact price.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign the listing">
        Sign the listing message in your wallet. Signing authenticates the listing; it is not an onchain transfer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Store the paywall link">
        Test the link, then share it with your audience through trusted channels.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Blockchain transfers are generally irreversible. Confirm the network, token contract, amount, and recipient in your wallet before signing.
</Warning>

## Testnet first

Developers and first-time testers should use Robinhood Chain Testnet. Testnet assets have no monetary value and must never be presented as mainnet funds.
